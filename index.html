Signal Integrity and System Design Hardware Engineer · AMD

Intern Sept 2022 – Dec 2023 · Full-time Feb 2024 – Aug 2026
  • Supported PCIe Gen6 (64 GT/s PAM4) SI sign-off on EPYC server platforms across four board topologies and two silicon milestones, covering twelve link directions.
  • Performed comprehensive analysis of channel features and topologies for implementation in Turin, Genoa and Venice server platforms.
  • Correlated a supplier card-edge connector model in HFSS to 32 GHz across six differential pairs, agreeing within 5 Ω on impedance, and root-caused a 0.06 mm mating gap that invalidated the geometry.

  • Ran corner and sensitivity studies that cut a channel-simulation campaign from 16.5 hours to 1 hour, with eye-height drift bounded under 3.5%.
  • Closed an intra-pair skew violation the layout tool's constraint manager had passed, bringing all lanes to about 1/5 of the spec limit.
  • Co-authored a 2D segmented DDR5 modeling method that matches full-wave eye margin within 5 mV and 3 ps without a layout file.
  • Built an automated channel-simulation QA tool that scores eye metrics, insertion loss and defect rate per lane and ranks equalization presets, replacing a manual screenshot workflow.
  • Reviewed three platform boards against the design guide and wrote custom DRC checks for differential-pair spacing and connector keepout.
  • Led development of a Signal/Power Integrity Platform Design Guide that set layout constraints and design standards adopted across multiple board revisions and engineering teams.
  • Extracted high-fidelity electromagnetic models for EPYC server platforms in HFSS, Q2D and Q3D to reduce crosstalk and impedance discontinuities on high-speed interfaces like XGMI and DDR5.
