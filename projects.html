DormPod

Senior design · JPods / City of Plano · 2023

An autonomous last-mile delivery robot, built as my senior design project with JPods and the City of Plano. It combines sensor fusion, motor control and safety systems, follows JSON maps, and coordinates with other pods over MQTT. We built small-scale and full-scale versions and moved from 3D-printed parts to a custom PCB.

Sensor compression device

EPICS / Autism Treatment Center · 2021

A medical-grade prototype built with the Autism Treatment Center: a cuff, a bit like a blood pressure cuff, that squeezes on remote command to give soothing, therapeutic touch. I took it through the whole hardware cycle, from picking components to 3D-printing the enclosures and laying out the PCB for the control system.

Magic Mirror

Murata Electronics · 2022

A customizable smart mirror with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, built around a Raspberry Pi and Murata components. It pulls live weather, news and calendar data through APIs. The idea had been on my mind for four years before I pitched it to Murata, got it sponsored, and started building a prototype for their Dallas office.
